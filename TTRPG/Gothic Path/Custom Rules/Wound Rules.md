
The basics.

Instead of HP, characters have wound slots. Each time they take damage a slot is filled with what happened and the amount of damage done. 

The "damage" is used as a DC for restoring the wound.

Each character has a number of slots for each type:
3+con minor, 2 Serious, 1 Grave, 1 Lethal
