**Injuries.** Whenever you take damage, you mark down an injury instead of reducing HP. An injury consists of 3 parts:
1. How you got it. "Stabbed in the gut"
2. How severe it is. "dc 8" (determined by damage)
3. Additional effects. "Bleeding 1d6"

**Slots.** Each injury fits in a slot, which slot depends on severity. If you're out of that type of slots, you put it in a more severe slot. Putting an injury in the lethal slot means you are dying and need to start making death saves.

Each character has the following slots:
Type     · Amount · Damage Threshold
lethal    ·           1 · hp x 3
grave    ·           1 · hp
serious  ·           2 · hp / 3
minor    · 3 + con

**Recovering** There are two ways to recover: treatment and healing
- **Healing.** Removes the injury if it beats the DC. This is spells and potions.
- **Treatment.** Removes side effects from the injury. This is non-magical.

**Dying.** You roll a death save immediately when you take lethal a lethal injury, and at the end of every turn until the injury is treated or healed. The save DC is the injury DC.
**Critical Success:** You only have 2 actions per turn. Mark one success.
**Success:** You only have 1 action per turn.
**Failure:** You are unconscious and have no actions.
