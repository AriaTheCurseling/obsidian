Aesthetic: **Gothic**

Locals wear **dresses with woven patterns that tell of the family's past achievements, each new achievement is added to the dress**.
#### Territory
Its borders extend to ..
- the **Theodananda grassland** in the West,
- the **Gelvira woodland** in the South,
- the **Matasuntha shrubland** in the East
- and the **Melle salt lake** in the North.

#### Politics
- Trouble: **Losing a war with [[Urbau]].
- Fortune: **A just leader is weeding out corrupt officials**.
- Dispute with [[Nomura]]: **They are suffocating the only trade route available to this kingdom with high border tariffs**.
- Positive tie with [[Urbau]]: **They took in this kingdom's refugees**.
