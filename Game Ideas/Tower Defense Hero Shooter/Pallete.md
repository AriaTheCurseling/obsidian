



##### Desired feel

strong interplay between multiple different gameplay loops

players control when its hectic and when its calm

resources matter, expend only what you need at every step





##### loops
###### Resource Collection
Finding and collecting resources
- Required for buildings to function
- 
###### Base building 
Building up a physical presence
- Creates conveyors for collection
- Supply players with tools
- Give players ammo and upgraded
###### Completing Objectives
Accomplishing various map specific tasks
- Grants buildings
- Summons enemies
###### Fighting
Killing things and defending yourself
- Dead enemies can be harvested
- Destroy buildings
- Interrupt objectives
