
##### Poison - Pain Killer
You feel no pain.
The gm no longer informs you how severe a wound is, or what mechanical effects they have. You are still affected normally by the wounds, and the gm still describes attacks and how wounds are received - You may see the enemy strike you and hear the sound of a bone snapping, but you wont know if its a critical or severe wound, or if it has any additional effects.
###### Poison - Blood thinner
Increase bleeding by 1
Faint earlier from bleeding
###### On Fire - Staged, Progresses end of turn
Application: Put "Ignited" onto equipment harm slot

End of turn 1: Inflict "Burning" harm on equipment 
End of turn 2: Ignited inflicts second burning, burning inflicts dmg on wearer

Stage 1: Minor equipment dmg, no character dmg - clothes on fire
Stage 2: equipment dmg, minor character dmg - growing blaze
Stage 3: equipment and character dmg - Blaze

Can be put out by going prone. Naked hairless creatures are immune.

###### Oil - Combo Status
On Fire:
- Removes immunity
- Immediately progresses fire by 1 stage.
