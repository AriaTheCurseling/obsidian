
3d20 system

The highest value determines **duration** 
The middle value determines **success**
The lowest value determines **cost**

The dice tell a story, if the duration is low, you aborted the action early out of caution, if the expense is high you overexcerted yourself. 

###### Duration
Signifies how long it took to finish. 

I'm thinking i want effects in combat at extreme values. Outside of combat is far easier - a crafting roll 
###### Success
Signifies how well you do. 

Compare your success with the difficulty of the action to find its success level. 

Outright failiure is rare, but a character might not get quite what they wanted. 
###### Cost
Signifies a price to pay down the road. 

This is tracked by adding a die to your character sheet; Use the biggest die size with fewer sides than your roll. 

1 - 4: no die is added
5 - 6: add a d4
7 - 8: add a d6
... 
13 - 20: add a d12
