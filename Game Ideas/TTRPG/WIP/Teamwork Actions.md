
You can let your allies help you during your turn.

Each ally can only perform one action per turn (except on their own).

Ask for help - Ask an ally to do something that directly involves you
> Essentially lets you use their sheet instead of yours for something
- Throw me at the enemies
- Give me a potion
- Protect me
- Move with me

Provide an opportunity
- Attack an enemy to distract them, giving your ally a free action
