Many systems utilize initiative systems to determine who goes when, what if initiative was a more involved system that could be increased or decreased during gameplay?

Initiative dmg: Make someone act later by feinting or magicking them slower
Initiative bonuses: Gain an advantage by doing various things

Opens the system up to general time magic

How to handle wrapping/double turns?

Highest initiative goes next until everyone have gone.
You get half a turn if you wrap around by gaining initiative
Your next turn is a half turn if you wrap by loosing initiative
