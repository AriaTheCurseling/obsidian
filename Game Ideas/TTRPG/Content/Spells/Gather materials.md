Ingredients:
> A recipe in mind
> The tools required at hand (Within unarmed reach)
> All Materials required nearby (Within movement range)
> Mobility of the caster (Reserved)

Infuse your mobility into the physical materials required for a recipe you know and have the tools to fulfill. They are telekinetically drawn to you, remaining within reach for the duration of the spell, where you may freely manipulate them.


Perk: Your control is greater than expected, gain a bonus to crafting speed
Flaw: The materials collect too fast, roll to dodge
