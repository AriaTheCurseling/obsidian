Ingredients:
> A recipe in mind
> The tools required at hand (Within unarmed reach)
> The Materials required nearby (Within movement range)
> Mobility of the caster (Reserved)

Infuse your mobility into the materials required for a recipe you know and have the tools to fulfill. They are telekinetically drawn to you, remaining within reach for the duration of the spell, where you may freely manipulate them.



Flaw: The materials collect too fast, roll to dodge
