


I have discovered the most wonderful spell. 

Never again shall i misplace or forget something of mine. I'm no longer capable of forgetting. 

Sounds unbelievable right? Me, incapable of forgetting? 

Remember how mother always joked i'd forget my own hands if they weren't attached to me? 

Every. Single. Day? 

Oh how often she repeated that phrase, how often i ignored it. 

but then, the other day i thought back and well, how come i never did forget my hands? why do i always know where they are? 

Well, i decided to try. surely its possible? 

And oh, isnt the answer wonderful. I never managed, no matter what i did, no matter which form of displacement i used, magic or mundane, i always knew of them, somewhere in the back of my mind. 

The rest of the story is self explanatory, i pulled on my anima, drawing out this innate knowledge and bound it to my belongings. 

One by one i knew where they where, a constant knowledge in the back of my mind. 

Of course, i forgot my hands for a while after that, but they've long since recovered.
