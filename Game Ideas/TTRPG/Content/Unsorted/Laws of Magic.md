

###### Laws of Animation
Animation requires the ability to do the skill you're animating

Animation can only do what you teach it

###### Laws of Enchantment
Enchantments fill the material - You cannot enchant half a rock
Enchantments compete - They are subject to repetition penalty
Enchantments fade without structure - But can be made to last
Enchantments can be dispelled - If you can figure out how

Laws of Space
Space and time are fundamentally entwined


###### Laws of Evocation
Evocation is the magical of manipulating energy
Heat, Gravity, Weight, Electricity, Kinetic, Chemical, Light, Sound

Dark > Gravity > Motion > Sound > Thermal > Chemical > Nuclear > Photonic
