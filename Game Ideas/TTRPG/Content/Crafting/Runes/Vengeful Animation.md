Collects the souled remains of scattered runes to animate an object or piece of terrain. The newly animated construct is hostile towards anyone who has recently destroyed a soulrune, acting against them on its own. It is friendly to its creator, and can be commanded to act against others than those who have wronged it, but does not do so on its own initiative. The construct has 2 actions per turn, acting just before its creator in turn order. If it is held, worn or otherwise in possession of a creature, any action costs 1 additional point.

Animates whatever it is inscribed upon. The newly animated creature has 1 action per round, and acts immediately before you in combat order.


Flaw: The animated creature is hostile to you
