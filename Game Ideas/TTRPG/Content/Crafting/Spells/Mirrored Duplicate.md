Ingredients
> Any target
> Its reflection... consumed by the spell
> A mirror that fits the target

Duplicate the target by drawing its reflection out of a mirror. 

Neither the target nor the copy have a reflection, but they are both mirror images of each other. Any damage, alteration or other change inflicted on one is mirrored on the other. 

If the target is a creature, they still move and think independently, but on the same initiative. They have advantage on empathy checks involving each other.

If the target is used as an ingredient in a spell, potion or anything else that would consume the component, then both are consumed. Both components can be used to cast two copies of the same spell or potion simultaneously with no issue but its near impossible to use them for different purposes.
