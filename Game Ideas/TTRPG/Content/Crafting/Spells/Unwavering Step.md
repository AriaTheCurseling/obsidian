1 minute.

Blesses a number of people with the stability and sureness of stone. 

The precarious stack of rocks topples over, each stone carrying with it a blessing from the stability and sureness that has let it withstand the forces of nature. The blessing remains in the stone for as long as the stack stood and spreads to anyone picking up a stone for as long as they keep it close.
