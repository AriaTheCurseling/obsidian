The inscribed object loses weight and substance but does not appear any different. Materials that started out more substantial are harder to get through.

Terrain: Anyone stepping on it might fall into it.
Walls: It is possible to push through, but not easy.
Armor: Requires less strength, hinders less, and is worse at stopping blows.
Weapons: An inscribed weapon is rendered blunt and almost rubbery.
Creatures: More flexible, can squeeze into smaller spaces, lower prowess
